Overview

Vision: In chaotic battles, players enjoy making mischief with their friends: the smugness of a successful backstab, the mix of laughter and frustration at being backstabbed, and the mutual "accusations" once a round ends.

Each player is a necromancer with a unique element. Everyone shares one goal: drive the Church out of the Dark Magic Theme Park. But harmony is not on the menu. Enemy souls are essential for casting spells, but a teammate's soul is far more powerful.

At the crucial moment of a fight, stab your teammate in the back, or even blow their head clean off: stronger attacks and more souls will be yours. Or make a small sacrifice and share your souls and power with a teammate for a while. Don't worry: a great necromancer can come back to life as long as their head finds its way back to their body.

Design Challenge

How can we get players in a co-op battle to choose to attack each other, and enjoy the backstabbing, teamwork, strategy and chaos?

  • Everything ends in laughter: fighting, attacking each other and dying all bring fun, not blame.
  • No burden, real rewards: attacking a teammate doesn't hurt the friendship, and it benefits both you and the team.
  • Everyone has a part to play: players of every skill level can find their own way to help the team.

Gameplay

Project Lich is a 3D top-down, 3-player co-op magic combat game.

  • Advance: players push forward through a level called the Dark Magic Theme Park and drive back every enemy.
  • Souls: casting spells costs souls, and defeating enemies earns more.
  • Attacking teammates: attacking or killing a teammate triggers an elemental reaction and grants a stronger attack.
  • Death: a player who dies drops the souls they carry and becomes a skull. As a skull, they can still grab souls, and they revive once they return to their body.

Core Loop

  1. Fight
  2. Gain souls
  3. Cast spells
  4. Defeat enemies
  5. Back to the fight

Death Loop

  1. Die in battle
  2. Skull flies off
  3. Find the body, grabbing souls on the way
  4. Revive
  5. Back to the fight

Systems Overview

  • Soul Resource SystemSouls are the casting resource: how they are gained, held, picked up and fought over.
  • Co-op Combat and Friendly FireThe core gameplay: attacking a teammate is a strategy with costs and benefits.
  • Rewards for Attacking TeammatesDamaging a teammate gives both players one elemental reaction attack; killing a teammate sets off an elemental reaction explosion.
  • Death and RevivalDeath doesn't take you out of the game: you become a skull, can still fight over souls, and return to battle after reviving.
  • ElementsEach player has a unique element.
  • Combat WavesA battle consists of 2 to 3 enemy waves: spawning, timers and early clears, and the end of the battle.
  • Enemy AIThe PoC has only one enemy type, which fires magic orbs.
