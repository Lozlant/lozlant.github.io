Systems design course assignment

Kingdom Rush 5: Progression Redesign

Hero-specific side quests that let players truly get to know each hero.

Role
Systems Designer
Team
4 people; led the systems design and documentation
Timeline
2024.11 – 2024.12
Subject
Kingdom Rush 5: Alliance (Ironhide Game Studio, 2024)

Overview

  • Set the system goals based on two player personas: the Commander and the Hero.
  • Designed 1–3 side-quest levels for each hero, using deployment restrictions and waves in stages to teach how to use heroes and towers.
  • Kept the total number of skill points the same, but turned the hero level 9–10 cap and some skill points into side-quest rewards; wrote the feature specification and configured levels and waves in Excel.

Design Goal

Help players truly get to know each hero through play: understand its strengths and weaknesses, know which towers it pairs well with, and learn its story. At the same time, serve two kinds of players: the "Commander", who enjoys strategic challenge, and the "Hero", who cares more about character growth and story.

Systems Overview

  1. Main LevelsProgress through the campaign to unlock new heroes and side quests
  2. Hero Side QuestsDeployment restrictions + tutorial waves in stages + comics before and after each level
  3. Completion RewardsUnlock the level 9–10 cap, earn skill points, see more of the hero's story
  4. Stronger HeroesBetter understanding of heroes and towers makes the main levels easier

The four steps follow one another and lead back to the main levels, forming a loop.
